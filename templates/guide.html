Setup Guide

How to connect Tri Tracker to your Strava account and import your training data.

{# ── Step 1 ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}
1 — Connect your Strava account

From the home page, click Connect with Strava. You'll be taken to Strava's website where you'll be asked to log in (if you're not already) and grant Tri Tracker read-only access to your activity data.

Tri Tracker requests two permission scopes:

  • read — your basic profile (name, profile picture)
  • activity:read_all — all your activities, including private ones
{# ── Step 2 ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}
2 — Import your activities

After connecting, click Sync with Strava in the top right of the Dashboard. The first sync downloads your entire Strava history — this can take 30–60 seconds if you have several years of data.

Each subsequent sync only fetches activities recorded since the last sync, so it completes in a second or two.

{# ── Step 3 ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}
3 — Keep your data current

Tri Tracker does not sync automatically. Click Sync with Strava whenever you want to pull in recent activities — once a week after your long ride is a good habit.

{# ── What we store ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}
What data is stored?

Everything is stored locally in a SQLite database on this server. Nothing is sent to any third party beyond the initial Strava API call.

Per activity we store:

  • Name, sport type, date and time
  • Distance, elapsed time, moving time
  • Average and max speed, elevation gain
  • Heart rate (average and max) if recorded by your device
  • Power (watts) if you have a power meter
  • Strava suffer score
  • GPS summary polyline (the encoded route — used to draw the map)
  • Start coordinates (latitude/longitude)
  • Indoor/commute flags
{# ── Troubleshooting ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}
Troubleshooting
Sync button says “Sync failed”
Your Strava access token may have expired. Try logging out and reconnecting — this gets a fresh token. Strava tokens expire every 6 hours but are refreshed automatically; a failure here usually means the refresh also failed, which reconnecting fixes.
Activities are missing from the list
The Activities page shows only triathlon disciplines (Run, Ride, Swim) by default. Use the All filter to see every sport type. If activities are still missing, run another sync.
The map shows no routes
The dashboard map only draws outdoor activities with GPS data — indoor trainer sessions and Zwift rides are excluded. If you only train indoors, the map will be empty.
